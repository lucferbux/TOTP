Privacy Policy

TOTP Password collects nothing. There is no account to create, no server of ours to talk to, and no analytics.

Effective 20 September 2026 · Last updated 20 September 2026 · Applies to TOTP Authenticator for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

In short

  • The developer receives no data whatsoever from this app.
  • Your two-factor secrets are encrypted on your device before they are written anywhere.
  • Sync is optional and goes only to your own private iCloud database.
  • The camera is used only to read QR codes. Nothing is stored or uploaded.
  • Face ID / Touch ID results never leave your device.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code.

Who this is from

TOTP Password is made by Lucas Fernández Aragón. Contact: GitHub Issues.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no user identifier, no device identifier, no crash reporting of its own, and no usage analytics. No data is sold or shared, because none is received.

What the app stores on your device

Accounts you add — the service name, the account label, the shared secret, and any fixed prefix (PIN) — are stored on the device. The secret and the prefix are sealed with ChaChaPoly authenticated encryption before being written to storage; the plaintext exists in memory only for the moment a code is generated. The encryption key is held in your Keychain and mirrored, readable only by you, inside the app's shared container so the widget and the AutoFill extension can generate codes too.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, accounts sync through the private database of your own iCloud account (container iCloud.com.lucferbux.TOTP). This is the one case where data leaves your device, and it goes only to Apple, on your behalf.

The developer has no access to that database: Apple does not give developers access to users' private CloudKit data, and the secrets and PINs are additionally encrypted with a key that exists only on your devices — shared between them through your iCloud Keychain — so what is stored is ciphertext the developer could not read even if it were accessible. Service names and account labels are stored alongside it so sync is usable. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera

The camera is used for one purpose: reading a setup QR code. Frames are processed on the device as you scan, are never written to disk and are never transmitted. You can decline camera access and add accounts by pasting a link or typing the secret instead.

Face ID, Touch ID and the app lock

The optional app lock uses the system authentication prompt. The app receives only a yes-or-no answer; your biometric data stays in the Secure Enclave and is never available to the app or the developer.

Clipboard

Copying a code puts it on the clipboard so you can paste it. Copies are removed automatically after the delay you choose in Settings, and stay on the device you copied them on unless you turn on sharing with your other Apple devices.

Widgets, Shortcuts and AutoFill

The widget, the Control Centre control, Shortcuts actions and the AutoFill extension read the same encrypted store on your device. Shortcuts and Spotlight see only service and account names — never secrets, PINs or codes. Nothing in these features transmits anything.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. It has no backend, no telemetry endpoint and no remote configuration. The only network activity is Apple's iCloud sync, performed by the operating system.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party libraries, SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks or crash reporters. It is built entirely with Apple frameworks.

Data Apple collects

Apple independently collects App Store download and purchase data, and — only if you opted in on your device — aggregate analytics and crash reports. Any such information the developer can see is anonymised and aggregated, and cannot be tied to a person. That collection is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA).

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Because no personal data is transmitted to or processed by the developer, there is no controller or processor relationship over your app data, and no copy of it to give you, correct or erase — the data is in your hands, on your devices and in your own iCloud account. Rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection are exercised directly there; see Deleting your data.

No categories of personal information have been collected, sold or shared, and there is no cross-context behavioural advertising, so no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link is required. If you email the developer or open an issue, whatever you choose to put in that message is processed only to answer you, on the basis of legitimate interest, and kept no longer than needed. You may complain to your local supervisory authority.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: swipe a code away, or use Select to remove several at once. Deletions sync to your other devices.
  • Everything on a device: delete the app. That removes its local storage.
  • Synced copies: deleting the app does not clear iCloud. Remove the accounts in the app first, or delete the app's iCloud data under Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage.

The developer cannot delete your data for you, because the developer has no copy of it.

Security and reporting a problem

Secrets are encrypted at rest and never logged. As with any authenticator, a device compromised at the system level — or an iCloud account someone else controls — can expose the data on it, so keep a device passcode set and two-factor authentication on for your Apple Account.

If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately through GitHub Security Advisories. You can expect an acknowledgement within five working days. Good-faith research is welcome and will never be met with legal action.

Open source

The app is open source under the MIT licence at github.com/lucferbux/TOTP. Every claim here — no network calls, encryption before storage, what is and is not synced — can be verified in the code.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the update is noted in the app's release notes. Previous versions remain in the repository's history.

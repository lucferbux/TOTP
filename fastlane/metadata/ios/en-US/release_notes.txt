Ready for iPhone Duo, and lighter and more reliable everywhere.

• Made for iPhone Duo: on the outer display the controls move to the side, and when you open it your codes fill the inner display in a grid that keeps clear of the fold. Folding or unfolding keeps your place in the list.
• Uses far less battery: codes stop refreshing whenever they're off screen or the app is locked.
• iCloud sync now keeps your latest change on every device, so renamed accounts, new PINs and updated secret keys show up everywhere. Accounts you delete stay deleted, even if you were offline.
• Counter-based (HOTP) codes are never handed out twice, even across devices.
• With the app lock on, widgets hide your codes and AutoFill asks for Face ID before showing your accounts.
• AutoFill only suggests an account on that account's own website.

TOTP Password comes to Apple Watch, and now speaks Spanish, German and French.

• Apple Watch: your codes on your wrist, each with its countdown. Add a complication to keep one account's code on the watch face.
• Codes reach the watch from your iPhone. Your PINs and counter-based (HOTP) accounts never go to the watch, and with the app lock on it shows nothing.
• The app is now available in Spanish, German and French, as well as English.
• Delete an account straight from its edit sheet.
• On iPad, drag a QR code image onto the add sheet to add an account.
• On the Mac: a new Accounts menu with keyboard shortcuts, Settings in tabs, sync status in the title bar, and QR code images you can choose or drag onto the window.

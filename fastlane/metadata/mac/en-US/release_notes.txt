A more complete Mac app, now in Spanish, German and French.

• A new Accounts menu: Sync Now (⌘R), and copy the code of any of your first nine accounts with ⌘1 to ⌘9.
• Select Accounts and Delete Selected Accounts (⌘⌫) are in the Edit menu. Esc leaves selection.
• Add an account by choosing a QR code image file, or by dragging one onto the window.
• Settings is now organised in tabs: General, Security, iCloud, AutoFill and About.
• The title bar shows sync status, and cards highlight as you hover over them.
• In the menu bar panel, search is ready the moment it opens: ↑ and ↓ pick an account, Return copies its code.
• Delete an account straight from its edit sheet.
• Widgets on the Mac now say "Click to copy".
• The Help menu links to TOTP Support and to Send a Suggestion.
• The app is now available in Spanish, German and French, as well as English.
• On iPhone, a new Apple Watch app puts your codes on your wrist.

NO ACCOUNT, NO SIGN-IN
There is no registration, login, user-generated content or paid content. The app works offline the moment it opens; no demo credentials are needed.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached to our reply in App Review (iPhone and Mac, latest OS, physical devices).

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
TOTP Password is a general-purpose two-factor authenticator for anyone who has turned on 2FA for their online accounts. It generates the standard six-digit codes (RFC 6238 TOTP and RFC 4226 HOTP) that services such as email, cloud and developer sites ask for at sign-in, on iPhone, iPad and Mac from one purchase. What it adds over typing codes by hand: AutoFill of codes into sign-in fields, widgets, a Mac menu bar item, private iCloud sync between the user's own devices, and an optional fixed prefix (PIN) for services that require a PIN typed directly before the code. It is not built for, or restricted to, any specific company or organisation.

3. HOW TO TRY IT
a) Copy this link, then tap + (Mac: + in the window toolbar) and choose Paste. It is the public RFC 6238 test secret, not a real account:
otpauth://totp/Example%20Corp:reviewer@example.com?secret=GEZDGNBVGY3TQOJQGEZDGNBVGY3TQOJQ&issuer=Example%20Corp
Alternatively type Issuer "Example Corp" and Secret Key GEZDGNBVGY3TQOJQGEZDGNBVGY3TQOJQ.
b) Tap Add. A code appears and refreshes every 30 seconds. Tap the row (Mac: click) to copy it.
c) PIN prefix: open the account, fill "Fixed Prefix (PIN)", save. The row shows a "PIN" badge (the PIN itself is never displayed) and copying now yields PIN + code.
d) Select (top right) enters multi-select for batch deletion.
e) AutoFill must be enabled once by the user: iOS Settings > General > AutoFill & Passwords > enable "TOTP"; macOS System Settings > General > AutoFill & Passwords > enable "TOTP".
f) Widgets: add "TOTP" from the widget gallery.
(The app is listed on the App Store as "TOTP Password"; on the device its short name is "TOTP".)

MAC APP
Opening the Mac app shows its main window. It also lives in the menu bar: the shield icon at the top right of the screen lists every code, and "Open TOTP" (Cmd+O) there brings the window back after it is closed. Closing the window keeps the app running in the menu bar; the Dock icon shows while the window is open and can be kept permanently in Settings (Cmd+,). The window stays hidden at launch only if the user turns on "Launch at login".

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Only Apple's iCloud: a private CloudKit database in the user's own iCloud account for sync, and iCloud Keychain for the encryption key. No servers of ours, no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, payment processors or AI services. Codes are computed on device with CryptoKit.

5. REGIONS
The app functions identically in all regions; there is no regional content or feature difference.

6. REGULATION / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated service and contains no protected third-party material. It implements open IETF standards (RFC 4226/6238); users add secrets issued to them by their own services. Only Apple-provided cryptography is used (export-compliance exemption, ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).

Lighter and more reliable.

• The menu bar app no longer uses CPU in the background: codes stop refreshing whenever their window is closed, minimised or covered, or the app is locked.
• iCloud sync now keeps your latest change on every device, so renamed accounts, new PINs and updated secret keys show up everywhere. Accounts you delete stay deleted, even if you were offline.
• Counter-based (HOTP) codes are never handed out twice, even across devices.
• With the app lock on, widgets hide your codes, AutoFill asks for Touch ID before showing your accounts, and the locked window can't be used from the keyboard.
• Copied codes stay on this Mac unless you allow sharing with your other devices in Settings.
